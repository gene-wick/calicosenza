About

Direction. Cali Cosenza Calcio is an independent supporters’ research project on Cosenza Calcio, written in Santa Barbara, California, by a fan with family roots in Calabria on his mother’s side. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Cosenza Calcio. It leads with one long piece, Exile at the Scida: Who Owns Cosenza’s Home?, on the stadium, public land and private rent. Think of it as a place, not a blog: a small archive of footnoted essays, each opening with an Italian abstract, under an original mark rather than the club crest.

Long, footnoted essays on Cosenza Calcio and the world it plays in: tactics, the history of Calabria and the Mezzogiorno, the labor and political economy of football, ultras culture and anti-fascism, and who owns clubs and stadiums and who pays for them. Short notes now and then, less academic and considerably less calm. On matchdays: short live notes on social; the longer take lands here after the whistle.

Standpoint. I write from below, about who owns what: the ground, the club, the rent. Somebody always pays for the stadium, and it is rarely the man in the directors’ box. So I read football as part of a social formation: clubs as capital, players as workers, stadiums as public land and private rent, the curva as a class institution, and the South of Italy as the product of uneven development, not of some southern character flaw. I say this once and openly so you know where the analysis comes from. The method is what has to convince you: documents, data, history and sources you can check.

How I work. Every factual claim is footnoted. Money, ownership and named people are sourced to primary documents. Every essay opens with a three-line abstract in Italian. Mistakes are corrected in public. No club crest, no club photographs I do not own.

The long game. These essays are drafts toward a book about Cosenza, Calabria and class.

Cali Cosenza Calcio è un progetto di ricerca indipendente e non ufficiale di un tifoso del Cosenza a Santa Barbara, in California, con radici calabresi da parte di madre. Non è affiliato al Cosenza Calcio. Si apre con Esilio al San Vito: di chi è la casa del Cosenza? Un luogo, non un blog: saggi con note e un abstract in italiano, sotto un marchio originale.
